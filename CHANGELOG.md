# Version history

Every version of Bible-Map, newest first. The site shows this file at [www.bible-map.com/versions](https://www.bible-map.com/versions). When a version ships, add it at the top; "Shipping a version" in the README says how.

## 0.5 (2026-10-22)

Why Bible-Map exists, and this version history.

- The About page opens with what Bible-Map is for: helping believers at every stage learn and grow, and helping anyone who doesn't believe see what we see.
- A version history: every version of Bible-Map, newest first, with what changed in each. It is linked from the About page, Settings and What's new.

## 0.4 (2026-10-15)

A new address, and writings.

- Bible-Map lives at www.bible-map.com. The old address forwards there and brings your settings along.
- Writings: answers to questions about the Bible, with every passage behind them linked and checked. The first is [How King Solomon Handled His Wealth](/writing/solomon-and-wealth).
- The list of writings takes the place of the notes under the search.
- Visits are counted with Vercel's own analytics, which uses no cookies.

## 0.3 (2026-09-21)

The Bible in the center, three new studies, and the name Bible-Map.

- The Bible sits in the center, with search on the left and maps on the right. At rest, the right pane maps the chapter you are reading; pick a verse to map where it connects.
- Back and Forward move to a bar under the header: its left half goes back, its right half forward.
- Maps go where you click: hovering shows what anything is, and a click goes straight there.
- New studies: [The Book of Isaiah](/study/isaiah), [The Trinity](/study/trinity) with a new triangle chart, and [The Resurrection of Jesus](/study/resurrection-of-jesus).
- [Studies](/studies) have their own page, sorted by theme and opened from one link under Explore.
- Notes under the search, kept in your browser.
- A new logo and a new name: Bible-Map. Search engines can now find every page.
- Clicking a verse no longer scrolls the Bible.

## 0.2 (2026-09-19)

Back and Forward, What's new, and four new studies.

- Back and Forward keep your place in the reader, and the reader keeps its place when you let go of a verse.
- What's new in the header, with a Coming soon tab, and New tags on new studies.
- On phones, the book button keeps the Bible open under the page, so a study and its text are on screen together.
- New studies: [The Presence of God](/study/presence-of-god), [The Miracles and Healings of Jesus](/study/miracles-of-jesus) with a Patterns chart that counts how they happened, [The Holy Spirit](/study/holy-spirit) with the gifts of the Spirit, and [The Promises of God](/study/promises-of-god).
- Long labels in study reference lists wrap, and the lists stack on narrow screens.

## 0.1 (2026-09-18)

The first version.

- Search people, places, topics, events and verses, and read the Bible in five public-domain translations: the Berean Standard Bible, the King James Version, the World English Bible, the American Standard Version and Young's Literal Translation.
- Every study can be seen as a theme map, a connection map you can zoom through, or a timeline; studies that link the Testaments also draw them as arcs.
- The meaning of every person's and place's name, with the Hebrew and Greek forms where they are known.
- Timelines for every person and for the whole story, and a page that puts two people side by side.
- [How the Bible is laid out](/bible): its books by size, the cross references between its parts, and an outline of every book.
- Nine studies: [The Kingdom of Heaven](/study/kingdom-of-heaven), [Christ in the Old Testament](/study/christ-in-the-old-testament), [The Parables of Jesus](/study/the-parables), [The Psalms](/study/psalms), [The Sermon on the Mount](/study/sermon-on-the-mount), [The Teachings of Jesus](/study/teachings-of-jesus), [The Tabernacle, the Temple and the Body of Christ](/study/tabernacle-and-temple), [Materials and Their Meanings](/study/materials-and-meanings), and [The Exodus and the Christian Life](/study/exodus-and-the-christian-life).
- A [feedback board](/feedback) that needs no account, an About page, and the [sources and licenses](/credits) behind the data.
